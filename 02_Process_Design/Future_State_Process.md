# Future State Process (TO-BE)

## Process Name

Automated Employee Onboarding Process

---

# 1. Overview

The future process uses RPA to automate employee onboarding activities.

The bot processes employee information, applies business rules, generates access requirements, and creates audit records.

---

# 2. Automated Workflow

## Step 1: Receive Employee Data

Bot reads onboarding spreadsheet.

↓

## Step 2: Validate Employee Information

Bot verifies:

- Required fields
- Data formats
- Duplicate records

↓

## Step 3: Generate Identity Information

Bot creates:

- Username
- Email address
- Temporary password

↓

## Step 4: Determine Access Requirements

Bot evaluates:

- Department
- Job role

↓

## Step 5: Assign Access

Bot determines:

- Security groups
- Licenses
- Applications

↓

## Step 6: Generate Communication

Bot creates welcome notification.

↓

## Step 7: Record Audit Information

Bot stores:

- Processing status
- Actions performed
- Exceptions

↓

## Step 8: Generate Reports

Bot creates:

- Success report
- Failure report
- Audit report

---

# 3. Expected Benefits

- Faster onboarding.
- Reduced manual work.
- Improved accuracy.
- Better compliance.
- Complete audit history.
